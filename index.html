Scoring Philosophy

XP Score — Raw Impact Index

The XP Score is a high‑resolution performance metric that captures a player’s impact on the field. It’s intentionally unfiltered — built from core stats like BA, OBP, SLG, BB%, and K% — and scaled so explosive players stand out.

XP rewards:

  • High batting average
  • Strong on‑base skill
  • Slugging power
  • Walk discipline
  • Low strikeout rates

XP is designed to pop. When a hitter has an Overall Score of 6.5 and an XP of 1100, it tells you he’s doing something special — even if his traditional stats don’t immediately show it. XP reveals outliers, breakout candidates, and fantasy sleepers.

Overall Score — 5‑Metric Skill Model (0–10)

The Overall Score is a balanced, normalized rating built from five core skill components:

  • BA — Pure Contact
  • OBP — Plate Discipline
  • SLG — Impact
  • K% — Decision Making
  • BB% — Patience

Each metric is scaled from 0–10 and weighted to produce a final Overall Score. This model reflects true skill and consistency — the kind of rating scouts and analysts rely on.

Why Both Scores Matter

XP shows impact.

Overall Score shows skill.

Together, they tell the full story of a player’s performance.

Current Research

XP Divergence and Fantasy Identity Stability

The ongoing research examines the relationship between a player's XP score and the XP expected from his Overall Score. Players are grouped by standard deviation bands based on their XP divergence, and subsequent Fantasy Identity changes are measured across those groups.

The current results suggest that players farther from their expected XP relationship may experience Fantasy Identity changes more frequently. This analysis is preliminary, and additional data will be used to determine whether the observed relationship persists over time.
